# Customer Tests: Interview Guide and Log

Three tests, 10 minutes each, on 22 Nov 2026. Goal: check our assumptions, not sell the product.

## Who to recruit

| # | Segment | Where to find them at the finale | Must have |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | New engineer (primary) | Other participants, IIIT Delhi students with internship or job experience | Joined a codebase they did not write in the last 2 years |
| 2 | New engineer (primary) | Product teams at the venue | Recently inherited a repository |
| 3 | New engineer (primary) | Product teams at the venue | Recently joined an unfamiliar team |

Interview an engineering manager separately to test the payer hypothesis.

Do not recruit teammates or friends who know the idea.

## The 10 minutes

| Min | Part | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 0-1 | Intro | "We are learning how people understand code they did not write. There are no right answers." Ask if we can take notes and quote them without their name |
| 1-5 | Their story, before any demo | Ask the questions below. Listen. Do not describe SkillForge yet |
| 5-8 | Demo | Show the live app. Let them type a question about Futon themselves if possible. Then show the three demo cases |
| 8-10 | Reaction | Ask the reaction questions. Write down their exact words |

## Questions (non-leading)

Before the demo:
1. Tell me about the last time you joined a codebase you did not write. What took the longest?
2. Think of a time you found code that looked odd. What did you do to find out why?
3. Who or what did you ask? How long did it take to get an answer?
4. Has someone who knew the reasons behind code left your team? What happened after?
5. (Manager) How do you hand over a project when someone leaves today?

After the demo:
6. What did you just see? Tell me in your own words.
7. Where would this fit in your week, if anywhere?
8. What would stop you from trusting an answer from it?
9. What would you use today instead of this?
10. (Manager) Who would decide to pay for a tool like this in your team? [Do not name our price first]

Avoid: "Would you use this?", "Is this a problem for you?", "Don't you think...?"
